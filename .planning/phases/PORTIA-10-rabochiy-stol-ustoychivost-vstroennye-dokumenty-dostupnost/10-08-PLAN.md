<objective>
Make «Свой» follow the colour the owner picked (UAT gap G-10-2b). Black, grey and white give a neutral grey theme, never teal. A dark or muted colour keeps its own hue and strength. Readability may only move lightness. This is owner decision D-23, which replaces D-05.

Purpose: the owner typed #0D0D0D and got a teal desktop. Their words: «сделать так, чтобы человек мог бы менять полностью цвет … если это черный, то делать там темно-серым, но никак не бирюзовым».
Output: a new deriveAccentTokens contract, one new token `--accent-chroma` that scales every accent tint, tests that pin the owner's rule, harness steps in the served boot script and in Edge, and D-23 recorded in CONTEXT and UI-SPEC.

Chrome tint choice (planner decision, as the orchestrator asked): a neutral pick makes the chrome neutral too. Every `oklch(L C var(--accent-hue))` tint gets its chroma multiplied by `--accent-chroma` (`:root` 1, so presets do not change). The other option, a faint tint left at some fixed hue, was rejected. The owner asked for the colour to change "полностью", and every one of the 422 sites has the same `oklch(<number> <number> var(--accent-hue)` shape, so one regex replace covers them and a test keeps them that way. Before planning, a headless Edge run confirmed that `calc()` works for the chroma and computes to `oklch(0.163 0 0)` at scale 0.
</objective>
