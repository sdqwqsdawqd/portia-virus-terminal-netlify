<objective>
Make the editor follow the tab list. Closed tabs vanish, their unsaved edits are reset with a message, saves never touch them, and a change by the owner reaches an open editor within one poll.

Purpose: PERM-03 («Редактор не видит закрытые для него вкладки»), the UI side of PERM-04 (the server's 403 is shown, not «ОШИБКА»), and D-14 to D-17. The owner's words: «человек, который не имеет доступ к разделу, не должен иметь доступа взглянуть внутрь».

Output:
- the desktop polls /api/access while the editor is allowed;
- the editor filters tabs, restricts history on closure and reverts closed sections in the save;
- a phase-12 browser harness with the `tabs` and `revert` checks.
</objective>
