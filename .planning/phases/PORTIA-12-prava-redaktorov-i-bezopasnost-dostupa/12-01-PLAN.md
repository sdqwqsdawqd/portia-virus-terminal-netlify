Purpose: SEC-01 must hold before any SQL. PERM-01 and PERM-02 need one shared tab list and one rule for "open", so the routes (12-05), the editor (12-06) and the chips (12-07) cannot disagree.

- the new `getEditorAccess` with fake-client tests;
- `/api/access` that answers 503 on a lookup failure;
- the D-19 login message.
</objective>
