<objective>
Replace the full-width «НЕ СОХРАНЕНО» bar with a compact chip and popover (EDIT-05; owner decisions D-09, D-10, D-11).

Purpose: the sticky bar covers the bottom of the editor and, on a phone, a large part of the screen. The owner wants a small counter that opens the details on demand, turns red when the save is blocked, and sits above the format sheet on a phone.

Output: chip + popover + live region in `components/portia-editor.tsx`; chip CSS with phone stacking; the old bar's CSS removed; harness check `savechip`; UI-SPEC amended.

Planner decisions:
1. A failed save (`saveState === "error"`) or a conflict also turns the chip red with «!». D-10 names only the blocked save. A chip that stays amber after a failed save would hide the failure the old bar showed in full text.
2. «✓» lasts as long as the desktop's existing «saved» state (1.8 s, `portia-desktop.tsx:1011`). No second timer is added. UI-SPEC said 2 s.
3. On screens up to 520px the chip sits at `right: 54px`, left of the ✎ button column (`right: 8px`, 38px wide), so the two never collide in any sheet state. The old rule that lifted ✎ above the bar is deleted.
4. The 99+ cap from UI-SPEC E3 cannot trigger: the count is the number of changed editor sections, at most 6.
</objective>
