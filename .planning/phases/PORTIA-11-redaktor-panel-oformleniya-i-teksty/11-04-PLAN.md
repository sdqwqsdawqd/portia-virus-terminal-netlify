<objective>
Make long editor lists easy to move through: collapsed cards, a table of contents with jump, and one-line names that never spill (EDIT-03; owner decisions D-01 to D-05).

Purpose: with many «Информация» sections the editor is one long scroll. The owner asked for:
- collapsed sections on entry;
- a TOC to jump;
- reordering without new buttons;
- a fix for long names that overlap the next section's buttons.

Output: `CollapsibleCard` and `EditorToc` in `components/portia-editor.tsx`, their CSS, aside-list ellipsis, harness check `sections`, and a UI-SPEC amendment.

Planner decisions:
1. Which lists collapse. Cards collapse; rows do not. The card lists with ≡ are the «Информация» sections, subsections and blocks. Сводка cards are added because the owner named «сводка» in D-02, though they have no ≡ today; no ≡ is added there. The lists with ≡ that are already one-line rows (the virus list, the Корзина list, the recommendation rows) are header-only by construction. They get the D-05 ellipsis and title; their rows already serve as the jump list. Mutations and scientist logs have no ≡, and logs are capped at 5, so they stay as they are. The owner confirms this reading in 11-07.
2. Collapsed bodies use the `hidden` attribute, not unmounting. Rich fields keep their DOM and selection state, and `aria-controls` always points at an element.
3. The TOC counts entries as sections plus subsections on «Информация», and cards of the shown category on Сводка. It renders at 5 or more entries.
4. The empty-state copy names the real button, «+ ДОБАВИТЬ РАЗДЕЛ». UI-SPEC wrote «+ РАЗДЕЛ».
</objective>
