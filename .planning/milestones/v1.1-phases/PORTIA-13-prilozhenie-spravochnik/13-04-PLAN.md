- :535-555 CollapsibleCard spreads cardProps on its root; the root is `#card-<id>` with data-sort-index; its body is hidden when closed.
- The guide tab from 13-03: card list `<div className="info-subsection-list">` per section (the empty hint is its only child when empty); section CollapsibleCards; cards with OrderDragHandle onMove inside the section.

- moveGuideCard(content, cardId, toSectionId, toIndex) clamps toIndex, so Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER means "the end".
- CDP drags: Input.dispatchMouseEvent mousePressed / mouseMoved / mouseReleased with button "left" produce primary pointer events.
